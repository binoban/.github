# Binoban

Binoban is an enterprise CDXP infrastructure platform for customer data, activation,
retail media, advertising, governance, and decisioning.

We build technology for large organizations that need to collect, unify, govern,
activate, and monetize customer data across enterprise environments.

## What Binoban covers

- Customer data infrastructure and profile unification
- Identity resolution and audience activation
- Engagement, journeys, and campaign workflows
- Retail Media and the Advertiser Portal
- Supply / SSP and Exchange operations
- Data governance, consent, schema, and quality operations
- Analytics, attribution, experimentation, and decisioning
- On-premise, private, and white-label deployment models

## Developer surfaces

Binoban SDKs, APIs, examples, schemas, and integration references are published here to
