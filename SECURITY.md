# Security Policy

Binoban takes the security of our software and our customers' data seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability in any Binoban repository, SDK, or service,
please report it privately:

- Email **security@binoban.io**
- Do **not** open a public GitHub issue, pull request, or discussion for security
  vulnerabilities.

Please include:

- A description of the issue and its potential impact
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- Affected component(s) and version(s)

We will acknowledge your report, investigate, and keep you informed of remediation
progress. We appreciate responsible disclosure and will credit reporters who wish to be
acknowledged.

## Scope

This policy applies to Binoban's public repositories and published SDKs/packages.
Customer deployments and on-premise environments are covered under their respective
support agreements — contact **support@binoban.io** for deployment-specific concerns.

## Please do not

- Publicly disclose an issue before we have had a reasonable chance to address it.
- Access, modify, or destroy data that is not yours.
- Run automated scans against production or customer environments.
